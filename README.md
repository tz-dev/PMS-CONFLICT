# PMS–CONFLICT

## Conflict as Stabilized Incompatibility under Binding, Time, Cost, and Exposure

**PMS–CONFLICT is a paper-specific application overlay of the [Praxeological Meta-Structure (PMS)](https://github.com/tz-dev/Praxeological-Meta-Structure-Theory)** — a generative operator framework (Δ–Ψ) for modelling praxis, asymmetry, temporality, distance, recontextualization, integration, and self-binding.

Where PMS–CRITIQUE reconstructs *critique* as a constrained praxis form, **PMS–CONFLICT reconstructs conflict itself** as a **terminal descriptive regime within this grammar**: a condition in which incompatibility persists and stabilizes once binding remains active, trajectories harden, distance becomes priced or asymmetric, exposure amplifies pressure, and reintegration (**Σ**) no longer carries.

PMS–CONFLICT does **not** treat conflict as a failure of communication, morality, empathy, or will.  
It treats conflict as a **structural outcome** of operator constellations under load.

The project builds explicitly on PMS as its canonical foundation and:

* introduces **no new operators**,
* modifies **no operator dependencies**,
* alters **no canonical order or derived axes**,
* adds **no prescriptions, interventions, or solutions**.

Instead, it shows:

* how incompatibility stabilizes once **Σ fails under Ψ + Ω + Θ with priced or asymmetric Χ**,
* why conflict is **not merely a temporary breakdown** but a legible structural regime,
* how **non-events (Λ)**, **recontextualization (Φ)**, and **attractor stabilization (Α)** can carry what Σ no longer does,
* why **distance (Χ)** becomes contested, priced, asymmetric, or delegitimized,
* how **publicness** and **misuse gradient** amplify drift without changing the PMS grammar,
* and why CONFLICT is a **terminal PMS-domain layer**, beyond which only downstream governance, institutionalization, decision, or evaluation remains.

## What PMS–CONFLICT Is

PMS–CONFLICT is:

* a **praxeological grammar of conflict** — not a resolution model,
* a **PMS_1.3-conform paper-specific application overlay** of the operator set (Δ–Ψ),
* a **strictly subordinate application profile** expressed in `PMS-CONFLICT.yaml`,
* a framework for analysing:

  * conflict as **stabilized incompatibility**, not merely escalation,
  * **binding collisions (Ψ)** and non-viable joint integration,
  * **cost topology** under asymmetry (**Ω**) and time (**Θ**),
  * **priced or asymmetric distance (Χ)** and exit realism,
  * **Λ-residue**, silence, delay, and missing closure as structural carriers,
  * **Φ-substitution** and **Α_conflict** stabilization,
  * **publicness amplification** and **misuse risk** without adding new operators,
* a descriptive model that evaluates conflict by:

  * operator carriage (what carries vs. what substitutes Σ),
  * distribution of continuous vs. irreversible costs,
  * exposure gradients, reversibility conditions, and misuse risk,
  * rather than blame, intent, pathology, or moral failure.

**Guiding formula:**

> **Non-moral in description /  
> Operator-strict in analysis /  
> Terminal in domain scope**

## What PMS–CONFLICT Is *Not*

PMS–CONFLICT is not:

* a conflict resolution or mediation framework,
* a communication or negotiation method,
* a theory of who is “right” or “responsible,”
* a diagnostic, psychological, or clinical account of actors,
* a justification tool for enforcement, exposure, coercion, or selection,
* a governance or policy manual,
* a license for person-level labeling or maturity typing.

Conflict is treated as a **structural regime** —  
not as pathology, immaturity, ethical deficit, or personal defect.

## Core Thesis

PMS–CONFLICT argues that:

* Conflict begins where **Σ-consolidation fails under binding** and does not recover.
* Continued integration attempts can **increase cost** rather than restore coherence.
* **Asymmetry (Ω)** and **temporality (Θ)** convert disagreement into persistence.
* **Distance (Χ)** becomes priced, asymmetric, contested, or delegitimized.
* **Non-events (Λ)** and silence can become load-bearing control channels.
* **Recontextualization (Φ)** can substitute for reintegration.
* **Attractor stabilization (Α)** can make incompatibility repeatable and durable.
* **Publicness** and **misuse gradient** amplify stabilization without changing grammar.
* Beyond a threshold, conflict becomes **legible but non-integrable** within this descriptive pathway.

> Conflict is not the absence of understanding —  
> it is the stabilization of incompatibility under cost, time, asymmetry, and constrained distance.

## Validity Gate (Application Only)

Any **application use** of PMS–CONFLICT remains gated by the canonical PMS entry condition:

* **Χ (Distance):** maintained reflective distance; no fusion into verdict, urgency, enforcement, or person-level attribution
* **Reversibility:** all readings remain scene-bound, revisable, and non-final
* **D (Dignity-in-Practice):** no shaming, ranking, humiliation, or ontological evaluation of persons or groups

This gate constrains **application only**.  
PMS itself may be criticized, revised, rejected, or ignored without satisfying it.

It is an **overlay**, not a fork.

* No operators are added or redefined
* No dependency relations are modified
* Dependency hygiene is preserved
* Reduced signatures are explicitly marked as shorthand
* Derived axes (**A, C, R, E, D**) are not redefined
* All claims remain **scene-bound, reversible, non-clinical, and non-diagnostic**

Where PMS provides the **grammar of praxis**,  
PMS–CONFLICT specifies how that grammar behaves when:

* incompatibilities persist,
* bindings collide,
* trajectories accumulate,
* exit and reflection become costly or asymmetric,
* exposure converts description into pressure,
* and reintegration is structurally unavailable.

## Terminal Position of CONFLICT

Within the PMS-domain stack developed here, **CONFLICT functions as the terminal domain layer**.

That means:

* PMS–CONFLICT increases the legibility of stabilized incompatibility,
* but does **not** generate a further internal reintegration grammar once Σ has failed under sustained Θ/Ω/Ψ with costly or asymmetric Χ,
* and any continuation beyond this point is a **mode shift** into downstream governance, institutionalization, decision, or evaluation layers.

This is a **grammar-internal limit claim**, not a universal claim that conflict as such admits no further analysis under other frameworks.
