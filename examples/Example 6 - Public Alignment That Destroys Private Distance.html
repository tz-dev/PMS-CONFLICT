2.3 Short scene

A public call for unity, solidarity, or immediate alignment appears under audience scrutiny. One role carries visibility and durable attribution, while another benefits narratively from public consensus without equivalent downstream exposure. Internal disagreement is not openly processed; instead, symbolic consensus accelerates because public records, timing pressure, and reputational coupling make delay or nuance costly. The system continues as alignment performance, but no protected private space remains in which Σ could still be reopened.

8.2 Case-specific validity test

Valid if

Drift signal: The decisive asymmetry is differential exposure to public consequence and reputational persistence, not moral superiority or deeper commitment.
